Plan, optimize and track every field sales visit — all inside Odoo 19.

Odoo 19 Community Sales Google Maps Route Optimization

Everything your sales team needs on the road

Stop juggling between Odoo, Google Maps and WhatsApp notes. Manage the full visit cycle in one place.

📍

City & Zone Filtering

Filter customers by city and a custom Zone field (e.g. "North Industrial", "City Centre") to build focused visit lists.

🔀

Manual or Auto-Optimized Order

Drag stops to reorder manually, or let the nearest-neighbour algorithm sort them by driving distance automatically.

🗺️

One-Tap Google Maps

Open the full route in Google Maps with all waypoints pre-loaded — works on desktop and mobile browsers.

✅

Visit Results

Mark each stop: Order Placed, No Order, Not Available, or Callback. Add free-text notes per visit.

📊

Visit History per Customer

Every visit is stored on the customer card. Managers see a full timeline of interactions without leaving the contact form.

🌐

Batch Geocoding

Geocode all customer addresses in one click using the Google Geocoding API. Coordinates are saved on each partner record.

How it works

1

Create a Route

Set the date, assign a salesperson and use city/zone filters to find the right customers.

2

Order the Stops

Drag stops manually or press Optimize Order to sort by shortest driving distance.

3

Hit the Road

Start the route, open Google Maps with one tap, visit customers and log results on the go.
